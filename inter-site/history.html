Since 1908

Our History

From a breakaway club in Milan to champions of Italy and Europe.

Born international

On the night of 9 March 1908, a group of football lovers gathered in Milan and founded a new club. They wanted it open to players from every country — and called it Internazionale.

More than a century later, Inter remain one of Italy's most successful clubs, known for dramatic comebacks, great captains and the unmistakable black and blue stripes.

Milestones

A century in black & blue

1908

The club is founded

Inter are formed in Milan after a split from AC Milan's parent club, built on the principle of welcoming foreign players.

1910

First Italian title

Just two years after their founding, Inter win their first Scudetto.

1928–45

A forced name change

Under the political climate of the time the club was merged and renamed Ambrosiana. The name Internazionale returned in 1945.

1964 & 1965

Grande Inter conquers Europe

Led by coach Helenio Herrera and a legendary side, Inter win back-to-back European Cups and the Intercontinental Cup in both years.

1989

A record-breaking Scudetto

Giovanni Trapattoni's Inter storm to the title with a points tally that became a club landmark.

1991 · 1994 · 1998

Three UEFA Cups

Inter become a force in Europe's second competition, lifting the trophy three times in eight years.

2010

The historic Treble

Under José Mourinho, Inter win Serie A, the Coppa Italia and the Champions League — the first Italian club to complete the treble.

2021

19th Scudetto

Antonio Conte's side end a long Juventus era and bring the title back to Milan.

2024

The second star

On 22 April 2024 Inter clinch a 20th Serie A title — and earn a second star above the badge — by winning the Milan derby.

2023 & 2025

Back in the Champions League final

Inter reach two European finals in three seasons, showing the club's continued place among the continent's elite.

2026

Champions again — Scudetto no. 21

Cristian Chivu's Inter wrap up the 2025/26 Serie A title with rounds to spare, adding a 21st league championship to the trophy cabinet.
