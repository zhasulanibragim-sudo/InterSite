About the club

More than a team

Inter were founded on 9 March 1908 in Milan by a group who wanted a club open to players of every nationality — which is exactly where the name Internazionale comes from.

The Colours

Black and blue — Nerazzurri. The colours are said to evoke the night sky over Milan, with stripes that are recognised around the world.

San Siro

Officially the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza, Inter's home is shared with city rivals AC Milan and is one of the most famous stadiums in football.

Nicknames

Nerazzurri, La Beneamata ("the beloved") and Il Biscione ("the big serpent"). Every Interista has a favourite.
